# Orbit Product Knowledge

Orbit is a project management and business analytics workspace. Teams use it to plan projects, track progress, understand business performance and automate recurring work. Current version: 2.3.1.

## Dashboard
The dashboard gives an overview of workspace activity: revenue, active projects, team members and the share of tasks completed. It also shows a 12-month revenue chart, a project health breakdown (on track, at risk, in review, completed), the active projects table and a recent activity feed.

## Projects
Users can create projects, assign team members, manage tasks and track progress. Each project card shows its status, progress bar, team avatars, completed tasks and due date. Projects can be filtered by status (All, On track, At risk, In review, Completed).

Opening a project shows its detail page:
- Progress, task count, due date and budget
- The task list, where tasks can be checked off, filtered (All / Open / Done) and added with the "Add a task" field
- The project team, including the owner
- The milestone timeline

To see how a project is performing, open it from Projects (or search for it) and review its progress, open tasks and timeline. Delivery performance across all projects is on the Analytics page.

## Team
The Team page lists workspace members with their role, team, location and online status. Members can be filtered by team (Engineering, Design, Product, Marketing, Sales). Use "Invite member" to add someone, or "Message" to contact a teammate.

## Analytics
Analytics provides revenue, user growth and project performance metrics.
- Revenue: monthly recurring revenue over time
- User growth: monthly active users
- Project performance: the share of milestones delivered on time per project, against an 80% target
The date range can be set to the last 3, 6 or 12 months, and data can be exported as CSV.

## Automations
Automations let users schedule recurring actions such as reports, customer follow-ups and reminders. Each automation has a trigger (when it runs) and an action (what it does), and can be paused or resumed with its toggle.

Current automations:
- Weekly Report: emails a workspace summary to leadership every Monday at 9:00 (Active)
- Customer Follow-up: sends a check-in email 7 days after a deal closes (Active)
- Project Deadline Reminder: posts to Slack 3 days before a milestone is due (Paused)

### How to create an automated weekly report
1. Go to Automations.
2. Click "New automation".
3. Name it (for example "Weekly Report").
4. Choose the trigger "Every Monday at 9:00".
5. Choose the action "Email report to leadership@".
6. Leave "Activate immediately" on and click "Create automation".

## Notifications
The Notifications page collects project alerts, automation results, team changes, billing events and product release announcements. Notifications can be filtered to unread and marked as read.

## Settings
- General: workspace name, timezone, theme (dark or light) and releases
- Notifications: email digest, mentions, project updates, weekly report email and product news
- Security: two-factor authentication, single sign-on (SAML), session timeout and audit log
- Billing: Enterprise plan ($4,800/month, 18 of 25 seats used) and the payment method

## Search
The global search box at the top of every page (shortcut Ctrl+K) finds projects, people, automations and pages.

## FAQ
**What can I do with Orbit?** Plan and track projects, manage your team, analyze revenue and growth, and automate recurring work such as weekly reports and customer follow-ups.

**How do I see my active projects?** Open Projects, or look at the "Active projects" table on the Dashboard.

**Where can I see revenue?** On the Dashboard (headline revenue and a 12-month chart) and in more detail on Analytics.

**How do I pause an automation?** Go to Automations and switch off the toggle next to it.

**How do I switch to light mode?** Settings → General → Appearance → Light.
