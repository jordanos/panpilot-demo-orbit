# Orbit Changelog

## Version 2.4.0: Smart Reports (released September 30, 2026)

### New: Smart Reports
Users can now automatically generate weekly business reports from their workspace data.

- Where to find it: Analytics → Smart Reports (route `/analytics/smart-reports`, pageId `smart-reports`). New items also appear in the sidebar with a "NEW" badge, and there's an announcement banner on the Dashboard.
- What it does: builds a Weekly Business Report covering revenue change (currently ↑ 12%), active user growth (currently ↑ 8%), project count and risks (24 projects), and written highlights about the week.
- How to use it: open Smart Reports and click "Generate Report". When the report is ready you can Download PDF, Send to team, or Regenerate.
- Scheduling: click "Schedule weekly" to deliver the report automatically every Monday at 9:00 to leadership@northwind.io. This replaces building a weekly report automation by hand.

### FAQ
**What's new in Orbit?** Version 2.4.0 adds Smart Reports: automatic weekly business reports generated from your workspace data.

**How do I set up a weekly report now?** The fastest way is Smart Reports: open it, click "Generate Report", then "Schedule weekly". You can still build a custom automation under Automations.
